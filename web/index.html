Controlled variation

Adventure changes only interior scale degrees. Low settings make occasional one-step substitutions; high settings change more notes and sometimes move two scale steps. Each Reply press advances to a reproducible new variation.

Protected identity

The source rhythm, rests, durations, velocities, first-answer anchor, scale, register, and tonic-triad ending remain fixed. Keep and automatic chord progressions remain later features.
