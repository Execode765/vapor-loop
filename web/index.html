Five timing characters

Original preserves timing. Pulse tightens notes to a sixteenth-note grid. Swing delays off-sixteenths. Pocket adds alternating laid-back placement and articulation. Half-time stretches the phrase toward twice its duration.

Honest scope

These are timing foundations, not claims of finished trap, R&B, or techno generation. Chord progressions, genre phrase grammar, added/removed notes, Keep, and richer synthesis remain separate milestones.
